FAQ

Questions, answered.

How long does a website take?

Most Starter sites go live in 1–2 weeks. Business sites usually take 3–4 weeks. Stores depend on how many products you have. You'll get a firm timeline before we start.

How does payment work?

50% up front to book your project, and 50% at launch. No hidden fees. The price we agree is the price you pay.

Do I own my website?

Yes. Your domain, your content and your site are yours. If you ever want to move on, I'll hand everything over.

Can I update the site myself?

Yes. On Business and Store packages you can edit text, images and products yourself, and I'll show you how. Or let me handle it with a care plan.

I already have a website. Can you redesign it?

Absolutely. I'll review your current site, keep what's working, and rebuild the rest so it's faster and easier for customers to use.

What do I need to get started?

Just a conversation. If you have a logo, photos or text, great. If not, I can help you put those together.
